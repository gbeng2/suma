# organizations rules

An organization is a suma partner, such as an affordable housing provider, that members belong to. A **membership** ties one member to one organization and is always in exactly one of three states: unverified (the member typed or picked a name), verified (staff or a registration link confirmed it), or removed. A **verification** is the staff to-do item that is opened for each unverified membership. A **registration link** is a link or QR code a partner shows to people so they become verified members of that organization automatically. Verified memberships matter because they are what gives a member the organization's programs. This page lists the rules the software enforces, what you or a member will see when a rule is hit, and where to fix it in the admin app. Every rule below was taken from the code at the file and line shown.
